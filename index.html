1. 0mm distance to edges and corners: Data comes from manufacturer. During corner cleaning, the distance was measured from the tip of the extended side brush to the wall, with a minimum achievable distance of 0mm. During edge cleaning, the distance was measured from the right side of the mop to the wall, also reaching a minimum of 0mm. Actual cleaning performance may vary depending on your home environment and conditions. Please refer to the actual experience.
2. Under refrigerators, door gaps and bed edges: Data comes from manufacturer, based on testing in a simulated home environment. When the height of gaps under furniture or appliances is at least 2.8cm, the robot vacuum automatically extends its mop arms to clean deeply. Actual results may vary depending on your home environment. Please refer to the actual experience.
3. 15,000Pa powerful suction fan: Tested with a vacuum gauge (range 0–35kPa, precision 0.01kPa) at the inlet of a fully charged robot vacuum, the maximum measured suction reached 15,000Pa. Data comes from manufacturer. Actual results may vary slightly depending on the testing environment.
4. >99% floor particle removal rate: Data comes from manufacturer. In Vacuum and Mop mode with Standard suction, Xiaomi Robot Vacuum H50 Pro removed over 99% of large particles, such as soybeans and rice, on hard floors. Actual cleaning performance may vary depending on your home environment. Please refer to the actual experience.
5. Dual anti-tangle system: Refers to the main unit's built-in "Y"-shaped comb teeth detangling technology and the anti-tangle side brush, which reduce hair wrapping around the main and side brushes during cleaning. Laboratory simulations mainly tested dry hair on the floor; cleaning performance for wet hair or fluff may vary depending on the home environment. Please refer to the actual usage experience.
6. Hot-air mop drying in as fast as 2 hours: Data from manufacturer. In a controlled environment at 25 °C and 55%RH, the mop was cleaned using the daily cleaning water volume and then set to drying mode. After 2 hours of continuous hot-air drying, the mop's moisture reduction reached over 99%. Actual drying performance may vary depending on environmental conditions.
7. 240m²: Data comes from manufacturer. In daily cleaning mode, the remaining water in the clean water tank was monitored every 10 m² of cleaning. Tests indicated that the vacuum can clean an area of up to 240 m².
8. Water tank images are for illustration purposes only. Please refer to the actual product.
9. As dust accumulation varies by household, the amount of dirt left on the mop pad after cleaning may differ. The base station's self-cleaning feature can help reduce dirt build-up and odours, but cannot eliminate them completely. Regular manual cleaning is recommended.
10. One bag lasts up to 75 days: Data comes from manufacturer. In a simulated home environment, a 100㎡ household with two people and carpets, where furniture occupies approximately 45% of the floor area, was cleaned once daily and the dust bag was measured every 4 days. Results showed the dust bag capacity could last over 75 days. Actual duration may vary depending on home size, furniture layout, and cleaning habits. Please refer to the actual experience.
11. Millimetre-level: The vacuum was tested in an automated sensor calibration rig. Four matte white panels were sequentially lowered at varying distances in front of the vacuum to measure the line-laser recognition accuracy. Results show millimetre-level precision, with a plane recognition error of ±6mm. Detection of obstacles depends on environmental conditions and usage. Actual obstacle identification and avoidance may vary.
12. Before the vacuum maps a new area, it is recommended to remove obstacles to ensure optimal performance. Obstacle avoidance effectiveness may vary depending on your home environment. Please refer to the actual experience.
13. Mop pads automatically lift by 10mm. For carpets thicker than 8mm, long pile carpets or carpets with fringes, we recommend selecting "Avoid Carpet" mode in the Xiaomi Home app.
14. Suction boost: After completing the first cleaning, select the top-right corner of the map on the Xiaomi Home app homepage. Tap Map editing > Carpet editing > Add carpet area > Carpet preference, and enable "Carpet Boost".
15. Designated furniture area cleaning: After creating a map, select the top-right corner of the map on the Xiaomi Home app homepage. Tap Map editing > Editing > Furniture editing > Add furniture. After saving, you can then tap the furniture on the map to clean around it.
16. Cleaning progress display: This feature provides a real-time estimate of remaining cleaning time based on the current task and remaining area. The estimated time is for reference only and may vary depending on environmental factors. Please refer to the actual situation.
17. 4 carpet cleaning options: Xiaomi Robot Vacuum H50 Pro supports Vacuum Carpet First, Carpet Avoidance, Carpet Crossing and Ignore Carpet. After the initial cleaning, go to the top-right corner of the map on the Xiaomi Home app homepage. Tap Map Editing > Carpet Editing, and choose your preferred option on the "Carpet Preference" page.
